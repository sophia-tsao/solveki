# Solveki demo: what to say

## 1. Google login

"This is Solveki. It's free math practice for grades 1–12 and AP, built on spaced repetition. It shows you each topic again just before you'd forget it."

"Sign-in is Google only, so there are no passwords to manage. The first time you sign in, you choose student or teacher. This account has already done that, so it goes straight in."

## 2. Available Courses

"Students choose what to review. There are 324 topics, from counting up to AP calculus."

"Ticking a course selects every topic in it. Changes save as soon as you click."

## 3. Diagnostic

"Students who don't know where to start can let Solveki choose for them."

"There are two questions about what you're studying, then three problems from units you've already covered. Solveki uses the results to decide which topics are new to you and which you already know."

## 4. Practice, then deck size in Settings

"Practice is a deck of cards. The problems are generated fresh each time, so students can't memorize answers. Topics that are due show up first."

"You get two tries. If you still miss it, you see the answer and a link to a video lesson, usually from Khan Academy. Solveki schedules the topic based on how you did. Right the first time means a longer gap before you see it again; a miss means you see it again tomorrow."

"Students choose how many cards they do each day, and the change applies right away. A new deck starts every day at midnight, local time."

## 5. Dashboard

"The donut shows every selected topic in one of four bands: New, Learning, Familiar and Proficient. The bands are based on how long the gap between reviews has become."

"The calendar shows the days the student practiced."

"Below that are the topics in each band, plus Shown soon, which is what the next session will draw from."

## 6. Classes

"A teacher gives out a six-letter code and students type it in. That's the only setup."

## 7. Assignments

"Joining the class picked up the teacher's assignment straight away. Its topics have already been added to my practice."

"Assignments don't need a separate worksheet. Every card I answer in normal practice counts toward the assignment. When I reach the card count, it moves to Done."

## 8. Teacher view

"This is the same app, signed in as a teacher. The chart shows the share of all students' topics in each band over the last 90 days."

"This is the class page. Each student has a familiarity bar, so you can see at a glance who's still mostly red and who's mostly green."

"This is the assignment report. It shows how many students have finished and the band mix on the assigned topics."

"Struggled with most lists the assigned topics with the lowest first-try accuracy, and how many students missed each one. That tells you what to reteach tomorrow."

"Familiarity by topic breaks that down topic by topic. Below it is a row for each student with cards done, due date and overdue flags."

"The test student we just used is in the list, and the cards they answered are already counted."

"This page shows one student. Under Practice you can see how consistently they've practiced over the last four weeks, their current streak, and for the last seven days, how many questions they answered, how long they spent and how often they got it right on the first try."

"Below that are the topics they've struggled with most this month, their assignments, and the same topic breakdown they see on their own dashboard."

"Making an assignment takes three steps: choose topics, set a card count, and choose classes. Once it's assigned, every student starts on it automatically."

## Close

"Solveki gives students endless practice that adapts to them, and gives teachers a live view of who's keeping up and what they're stuck on. And it's free."
